Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Absolute Client-Side Keystroke Privacy

Your privacy while typing is non-negotiable. Monkeytype Pro executes 100% inside your local web browser. None of the keys you press, the words you complete, or your typing cadence are recorded, monitored, or transmitted to remote servers.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect personal identifying information (PII). We do not require registration, email addresses, or social logins:

  • No Keyloggers: Keystroke data is held transiently in volatile browser memory and immediately discarded.
  • Local Storage Only: Any customized settings (such as test duration preference) remain confined to your local device.
  • No Advertising Cookies: We run no invasive behavioral advertising tracking networks.

3. Compliance Standards

Because no personal information leaves your terminal, our platform inherently complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global digital security standards.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding the technical structure of our typing engine, you can reach out via email at privacy@example.com.
