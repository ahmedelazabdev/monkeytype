Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 2026

1. Absolute Client-Side Keystroke Privacy

Your privacy while typing is non-negotiable. Monkeytype Pro executes 100% inside your local web browser runtime. None of the keys you press, the words you complete, or your typing cadences are recorded, monitored, or transmitted to remote cloud databases.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect personal identifying information (PII). We do not require registration, email addresses, or social accounts:

  • No Keyloggers: Keystroke buffer data exists strictly in volatile device RAM and is discarded as soon as the test finishes or is refreshed.
  • Local Storage Isolation: Any saved preferences (such as chosen duration or theme state) are retained solely within your local device's localStorage.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Trackers: We do not deploy behavioral tracking pixels or sell telemetric usage datasets.

3. Regulatory Compliance

Because zero personal data leaves your terminal, our service architecture naturally meets the compliance requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global digital security frameworks.

4. Technical Questions

For inquiries regarding our zero-overhead client execution model or security parameters, reach out directly via privacy@example.com.
