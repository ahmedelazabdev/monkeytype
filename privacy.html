Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Absolute Keystroke Privacy

Unlike cloud typing software that transmits keypress data to remote servers for telemetry, TypeSpeed Pro executes 100% inside your local browser runtime. None of the text you type, backspace inputs, or timestamps ever leave your device.

2. Data Collection and Cookies

TypeSpeed Pro does not sell, rent, or trade user data. Any potential preference data (such as selected test durations or personal best scores) is retained exclusively inside your browser's localStorage.

3. Third-Party Analytics

We avoid invasive behavioral trackers. Should lightweight analytics be utilized, they are aggregated, anonymized, and comply fully with GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this policy or our technical architecture, contact us at privacy@example.com.
