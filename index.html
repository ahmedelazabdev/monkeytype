  • Standardized Scoring: Speed is calculated using international normalized formulas rather than arbitrary raw word counts.

How WPM & Accuracy Are Formulated Mathematically

To ensure parity with professional typing certifications, words are normalized to exactly 5 characters per word (including spaces and punctuation). This prevents shorter vocabulary from artificially boosting scores:

Gross WPM = (Total Keystrokes Entered / 5) / Time Elapsed (Minutes) Net WPM = Gross WPM - (Uncorrected Errors / Time Elapsed) Accuracy = (Correct Keystrokes / Total Keystrokes Pressed) * 100%

Essential Touch Typing Techniques to Break Past 100 WPM

  1. Anchor on the Home Row: Keep your index fingers anchored on the tactile bumps of the F and J keys. Return to this position naturally after executing distant reaches.
  2. Do Not Look Down: Looking at the keyboard destroys neuromuscular memory. Force your brain to map key distances purely through physical proprioception.
  3. Prioritize 98% Accuracy Over Speed: Incurring mistakes causes an immediate penalty to your Net WPM because backspacing wastes precious seconds. Maintain high accuracy to establish rhythmic flow.

Frequently Asked Questions About Monkeytype

What makes Monkeytype the preferred typing test format?

Monkeytype revolutionized typing tests by removing visual clutter, banners, and slow animations, giving users a smooth caret, live feedback, and keyboard-first shortcuts to foster uninterrupted focus.

How is WPM calculated in this Monkeytype alternative?

Gross WPM is calculated as (Total Keystrokes / 5) / Time in Minutes. Net WPM subtracts uncorrected errors to give an accurate, standardized assessment of true typing velocity.

Does this tool track or save what I type?

No. All text validation and metric calculations occur locally in your web browser's memory. No keystroke logs or personal inputs are transmitted to external servers.
