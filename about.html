About TypeSpeed Pro

TypeSpeed Pro was created out of a desire for an unobstructed, latency-free typing environment that combines minimalist design with deep performance analytics.

Zero-Latency Architecture

Most online typing tests suffer from DOM bloat, heavy third-party tracker overhead, and inefficient rendering loops that add millisecond delays to keypresses. TypeSpeed Pro uses a lightweight, vanilla execution stack that captures and resolves keystrokes at your monitor's highest refresh rate.

The Philosophy of Precision Over Raw Speed

True typing velocity is useless if uncorrected mistakes compromise code or text clarity. Our measurement framework penalizes habitual errors and promotes ergonomic, steady motor rhythm that preserves long-term joint health while pushing typists beyond the 100 WPM threshold.

Open, Private, and Modern

We believe essential productivity tools should respect user privacy. We don't record key logs, capture personal text, or require intrusive accounts to experience the full software suite.
