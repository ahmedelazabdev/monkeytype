About Monkeytype Pro & Scientific Typing

Monkeytype Pro is an open-access, zero-overhead typing speed benchmark designed to provide typists with an unhindered test of pure neuromuscular coordination. We engineered this platform to resolve the flaws of legacy typing tests that suffer from high input latency, cluttered screens, and invasive trackers.

The Zero-Latency Architecture

At velocities beyond 100 WPM, a typist enters over 8 to 10 characters every second. Traditional web apps built on heavy reactive frameworks often introduce frame drops and input queuing. Our typing engine processes keypresses directly through vanilla event listeners, updating DOM state synchronously in under 5 milliseconds.

Standardized WPM Metrics

Natural language words differ significantly in character count. To preserve statistical accuracy across varying vocabularies, we adhere strictly to the international standard defining 1 normalized word as exactly 5 keystrokes (including spaces). This ensures your score reflects real typing speed, not vocabulary luck.

Our Core Design Philosophy

We believe web productivity utilities should be quiet, aesthetically unobtrusive, and fiercely protective of attention. You will never encounter interstitial video ads, intrusive paywalls, or forced account creation screens on Monkeytype Pro.
