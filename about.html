About Monkeytype Pro & Scientific Typing

Monkeytype Pro is an open-access, zero-overhead typing test designed to provide typists with an unhindered benchmark of keyboard agility. We developed this platform to overcome the limitations of legacy typing tests that fail to offer accurate latency representation or modern privacy standards.

The Zero-Latency Architecture

Typing at speeds exceeding 100 WPM translates to more than 8-10 keystrokes per second. Even a 20-millisecond input lag creates neuromuscular friction. Our engine bypasses heavy frameworks, computing every key event directly in the native browser loop to guarantee sub-millisecond reaction times.

Standardized WPM Metrics

Words come in varying lengths. To make test results scientifically comparable across sessions, we implement the international standard wherein every 5 keystrokes (including spaces) constitutes one normalized "word." This provides an objective, consistent assessment of true motor speed.

Our Core Design Philosophy

We believe web tools should be fast, aesthetically minimal, and respectful of human focus. You will never encounter interstitial advertisements, paywalls, or forced account creation prompts on Monkeytype Pro.
